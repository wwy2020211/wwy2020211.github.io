### 1. Understanding the Hamiltonian from an Engineering Perspective

If you are familiar with transfer functions, input–output systems, or state-space models, you already have a useful foundation for understanding the Hamiltonian in quantum mechanics.

These notes introduce the Hamiltonian through a simple two-input, two-output system, explaining the physical meaning of matrix elements, their role in quantum dynamics, and their geometric interpretation.

**The goal is not to memorize matrix notation, but to understand what each term actually does.**
